MRGARGSIR HEWP Edge Extension

A free auxiliary utility that smooths out rough edges on the HEWP portal — smarter search, one-click checkboxes and a fix for the Enter-key form reset bug.

🔧 Main Features

Important Note: All core portal automation (uploading estimates, e-MB bills, variations, and downloading data) is built directly inside our Excel Tool. This browser extension is an extra, optional, lightweight utility.

Smart Tender & Dropdown Search

Enhanced search for any dropdown on the HEWP portal, with filtering options — find Tender, HSR and other long dropdowns instantly.

Toggle All Checkbox

Select or deselect all checkboxes on a portal page with a single click.

Enter Button Glitch Fix

Pressing Enter will no longer clear your form on the HEWP portal — a long-standing portal bug, fixed.

Important Bookmarks

Adds advanced, ready-made bookmarks for faster navigation across the HEWP portal.
