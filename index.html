Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why use the Estimation & Billing HEWP Excel Tool?

It saves hours on template preparation and variation work, removes manual mapping errors and supports full bill scraping and comparison. Importing PDFs, auto-uploading e-MBs, estimates and variations alone can cut your work time by 90%. Built district-by-district for engineers, JEs and contractors.

Is the Excel Tool free to use?

It offers a 30-day free trial with full access to every feature. After the trial, premium features require a license. The Chrome/Edge extension stays free either way.

Does the browser extension upload to the portal?

No. All estimate/bill creation, PDF importing, data scraping, variation calculation and auto-uploading happen inside the Excel Tool. The browser extension is a separate, auxiliary utility that fixes portal UI glitches and adds shortcuts.

Which Excel versions are supported?

Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 and Microsoft 365 — both 32-bit and 64-bit.

Can I use this for both Contractor and Employee logins?

Yes — it's designed to work with both login types on the HEWP portal.

How do I update the tool?

Auto-updates are on by default. You'll be notified of major new versions, and can also check manually from the Tool Settings tab.

Are my login credentials safe with fast-login?

Yes — credentials are stored locally on your computer and are only ever sent to the official HEWP portal, never to any external server.

Can I use this on multiple computers?

You can install it on as many computers as you like, but each one needs its own license. Reach out if you'd like to discuss an arrangement for multiple devices.
